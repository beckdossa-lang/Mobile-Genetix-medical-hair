Frequently Asked Questions

A few common questions about Mobile Genetix Medical Hair™.

What is a cranial prosthesis?

A cranial prosthesis is a customized hair system designed for individuals experiencing significant hair loss. Options can be personalized for fit, appearance, texture, density and lifestyle needs.

Who may benefit from medical hair solutions?

Medical hair solutions may be appropriate for individuals experiencing hair loss associated with conditions such as alopecia, medical treatment, autoimmune conditions, genetic hair loss and other causes. A consultation helps determine what solution may be appropriate for your individual needs.

Do you offer human-hair solutions?

Yes. Human-hair options may be available depending on the client's needs, desired specifications and selected solution.

Can my hair solution be customized?

Yes. Customization may include factors such as size, color, texture, length, density and construction.

Do you accept insurance?

Coverage and reimbursement vary by insurance plan and individual circumstances. During your consultation, we can discuss documentation and next steps related to your specific situation. Insurance coverage is not guaranteed.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes. Virtual medical hair consultations are available for clients who prefer a remote consultation experience.

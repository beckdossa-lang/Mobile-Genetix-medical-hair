Who is Mobile Genetix Medical Hair™ for?

Our services are designed for individuals experiencing hair loss related to alopecia, medical treatment, autoimmune conditions, genetic hair loss and other causes.

Do I need a consultation?

A consultation allows us to understand your goals and help identify the appropriate solution for your needs.

Can I schedule a virtual consultation?

Yes. Virtual medical hair consultations are available for clients who prefer to begin remotely.

Can I submit my medical records through the website?

No. Please do not submit medical records or other sensitive medical information through our basic online request form. Detailed information can be discussed during your consultation.

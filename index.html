Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? Start here.

Who are medical hair solutions for?

Medical hair solutions may be appropriate for individuals experiencing hair loss associated with alopecia, medical treatment, autoimmune conditions, genetic hair loss and other causes. A consultation helps determine appropriate options for each individual.

What is a cranial prosthesis?

A cranial prosthesis is a specialized hair-replacement solution designed for individuals experiencing significant hair loss. Your consultation can help determine whether this type of solution may meet your needs.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes. Virtual medical hair consultations are available for clients who prefer to meet remotely.

Do you accept insurance?

Insurance coverage varies by plan, diagnosis and individual circumstances. Coverage should be verified before services are provided.

How do I get started?

Begin by selecting the consultation that best fits your needs and follow the booking process.
